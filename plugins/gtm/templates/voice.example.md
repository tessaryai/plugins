# Voice profile

/gtm:setup copies this file to the voice file (the `voice` path printed by `config.py paths`) and fills it with you. /gtm:campaign-write reads it before it drafts any copy. Replace every line in angle brackets. Real sample messages you wrote yourself teach more than any rule below.

## Who is writing

- Name and role: <Your Name, founder of Example Co>
- What you sell, in one sentence a prospect would understand: <...>
- Who you write to: <roles and company types, see icp.md>

## Tone

- <Direct and friendly. Peer to peer, not salesperson to buyer.>
- <Confident without hype. Plain claims you can back up.>
- <Curious: ask one real question.>

## Shape

- Sentence length: <short, mostly under 15 words>
- Message length: <LinkedIn invite note: none. LinkedIn message: under 60 words. First email: under 90 words. Follow-up: under 50 words.>
- Paragraphs: <one to two sentences each, blank line between>
- Opening: <start with them or a specific observation, never with "I hope this finds you well">
- Call to action: <one low-effort ask, for example "Worth a 15-minute call next week?">
- Formatting: <plain text, no bold, no emoji, no bullet lists in first touches>

## Words

- Use: <words you actually say, product terms you prefer>
- Never use: <"synergy", "circle back", "touch base", "quick question" as a subject, "I'd love to", "game-changer", "revolutionize", "leverage", exclamation marks>
- Names: <how you refer to your company and product>

## Sign-off

Write one line. campaign-write ends every email with exactly this text, on its own lines after the body. Use `/` for a line break (`Best, / Your Name` becomes two lines). Write `none` for no sign-off. An empty or unfilled line also means no sign-off.

Sign-off: <Best, / Your Name, or none>
